THE PAVONE PHILOSOPHY

WE TAKE THICKSHAKES
SERIOUSLY.

We started Pavone with one uncompromising rule: a thickshake should never taste like flavored iced milk. It should be dense, velvety, and luxurious enough to eat with a spoon.

01 / The Dairy Standard

Zero Added Water. 100% Whole Velvet.

Most milkshakes lose their integrity within 3 minutes because they rely on crushed ice. At Pavone, our base is made solely with ultra-rich dairy milk cream churned at precise temperatures. The result is pure, unadulterated decadence that stays thick till the very last scoop.

02 / Gourmet Sourcing

Belgian Couvertures & Real Mahabaleshwar Berries.

From authentic Lotus Biscoff speculoos spreads imported from Belgium to slow-roasted whole cashews and real fruit purées, we source the highest grade ingredients without cutting corners.
